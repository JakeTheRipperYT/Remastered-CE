Dynamic campaign-battle weather contract — retained behavior

Scope: campaign battles only. Custom and historical battles retain their native weather behavior.
The forecast preserves the initially rolled weather. Waiting retains the native three-Wait limit and updates the forecast; no extra rerolls are introduced.
The plan uses a private deterministic seed, preserves actual gameplay time of day, and remains independent of the local Always Night visual preference and shared engine RNG.
Use seasonally valid native states: rain/fog/clear outside winter, snow/fog/clear in winter, restricted to available scenes. Some battles stay steady; multi-state forecasts repeat, rather than stopping permanently at the last entry.
Use battle simulation time for progression. Existing policy uses 180–240 second phase spacing and 30–60 second fades.
Sky textures, environment lighting/colours, fog, precipitation density, vegetation wind strength and ambient weather effects transition gradually on the shared weather progress. Precipitation must remain present after a completed transition until the next weather change.
Preserve time of day, sun Euler direction and sky heading across weather changes, and preserve Always Night functionality. Avoid repeatedly rebuilding sky jobs during a fade.
Rain/storms retain native lightning and thunder handling, with guards against replacing the transition envelope during a fade.
Update native climate/database weather gameplay effects along with the visual transition. Unit cards show native weather-adjusted Accuracy and Reload Skill, refresh while open, restore in clear weather, and preserve fatigue/other bonuses without stacking.
Opening ground is immutable for the battle: rainy start retains the opening puddle coverage, shape, material and grass-cull policy even after clearing; clear start never gains puddles when later rain begins. Later sky/precipitation must not choose a new ground policy. Preserve this through deployment/Start Battle, steady frames and shader-resource refreshes.

Grass correction 0.1.71: retain matte dielectric base, then apply the wet coating using the campaign opening-ground flag rather than the currently selected dry/rain grass shader. Native fallback remains for unmanaged battles. The engine/helper and terrain assets remain identical to 0.1.70. Hardware and WARP execute the actual grass GBuffer shaders in 36 cases each; native transition fixtures cover 96 scenarios and named weather-buffer registration at three bases. In-game terrain-puddle appearance still requires confirmation.
